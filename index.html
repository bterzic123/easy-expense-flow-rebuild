What this is

We opened Easy Expense the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Easy Expense: installed it, went through every screen up to the plans, read your App Store listing and your website, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you currently show people.

Your onboarding is already good. It asks real questions and your write-off loader actually uses the answers. What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: your blue, your emoji cards, your write-off loader, your free-trial timeline and your navy Professional card. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment. This is not a design pitch.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
