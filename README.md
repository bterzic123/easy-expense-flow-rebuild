# Growth Audit — Easy Expense

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/easy-expense-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Easy Expense already has, each rebuilt as a working screen in Easy Expense's own design. Prepared by Adapty, October 2026, based on the live iOS flow, the App Store listing and easy-expense.com.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Give the paywall an X, and one offer behind it: Starter at €8.63 a month, next to what Professional adds | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Name their industry and their write-off categories in the paywall headline | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Ask for the account after the paywall, not on screen two | Sign-up | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 04 | Put the 4.9 from 2.5K App Store ratings and “over $500 million” found on the paywall | Paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Show the iOS tracking prompt after sign-up, not one tap before the price | Sign-up | CR +10–15% |

Combined potential: CR to purchase +25%, ARPU +30%.

## Grounding

Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from Easy Expense's own App Store listing, website and in-app screens. Prices are shown in euros, exactly as the app displayed them on our test device. Only the order of screens changed; the art direction is Easy Expense's.

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Easy Expense.
